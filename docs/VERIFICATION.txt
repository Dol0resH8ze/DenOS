Limits
- Physical computers, USB writing and hardware-specific drivers were not tested.
- The optional Windows VirtualBox launcher received syntax and CLI flag checks;

- Secure Boot is unsupported. Debian main-only drivers/firmware are included.
- The installed filesystem is unencrypted. The installer erases its chosen disk.
- This is an experimental remix, not an independently audited anonymity system.
